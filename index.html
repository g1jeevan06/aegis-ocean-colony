FIELD GUIDE

How to play AEGIS

You are the relief engineer sent to AEGIS, a research colony floating in the middle of the Pacific. It went silent in a storm 36 hours ago and its commander is missing. Walk around, follow the orange marker, listen to the radio, and decide what to send home at the end. There is no combat and you cannot die. If you fall in the sea, a drone fishes you out.

Basics

  • Move W A S D · look with the mouse · sprint Shift · jump Space · crouch C
  • Use things with E. Big actions (consoles, the scanner, the breaker, the uplink) need you to hold E until the ring fills.
  • Your objective is top-left. The orange marker on the compass, minimap and map points to it, with the distance and ▲/▼ if it is on another floor.
  • Map M has tabs for each floor and the whole site. Journal J keeps every data log and every radio message you have heard.
  • The glass lift is in the entrance lobby. Stand inside and press 1 lobby, 2 Mission Control / gallery, 3 roof.
  • Radio messages play with voices and subtitles. They pause while a menu is open. Change Voice volume in Settings.
  • Press Space or SKIP to skip the opening and ending films.

Walkthrough

Spoilers. Open one chapter at a time.

CH 1Silent Water
  1. From the landing pad, walk north over the lit bridge into the entrance lobby.
  2. Take the glass lift to floor 2. Mission Control is on this level.
  3. Stand at the main console and hold E to restart the command bus. The colony comes back online.
CH 2Clearance
  1. Go back down to Level 1 and into the Crew Lounge. Commander Reyes' orange Level-3 keycard is on a table. Press E to take it.
  2. Follow the ring corridor to the red door of the Cryogenic Laboratory. It opens now.
  3. Shortcut: the stair in Engineering goes down to the service ring, and a secret stair there leads up into the cryo lab.
CH 3The Cold Room
  1. Walk up to the primary cryo pod and hold E to scan specimen AX-7.
  2. The scan wakes it up. An alarm starts and the lights flicker.
CH 4Heartbeat
  1. Go to Engineering on Level 1, the room with two big generators.
  2. Find the dark breaker cabinet with yellow stripes and a red light on the wall, then hold E to reset breaker bank 3. The alarm stops.
CH 5Moon Pool
  1. Leave the colony and cross the bridge to the Marine Dock, the platform with the yellow crane and the submarine.
  2. By the railing of the moon pool there is a glowing data log on a small crate. It is Reyes' suit recorder. Read it.
  3. Look out to sea past the dock. Something is glowing in the water, pulsing like a heartbeat.
CH 6The Choice
  1. Take the lift to floor 3, or climb the terrace stairs, to reach the Observation Roof.
  2. Go to the uplink console next to the dish and hold E.
  3. Choose: Send everything (obey Shore Command, AX-7 is frozen for good) or Send the science only (let AX-7 go home to its mother).
  4. Each choice has its own ending film. Play again from the title and pick the other one to see both.
  5. After the ending you can press Keep exploring and walk around freely.

Hidden areas

SECRETSTwo places most people miss
  • Service ring: in Engineering, look for the amber SERVICE LEVEL ▼ sign and take the stairs down. A ring of tunnels runs under the whole deck. The unofficial stair up into the cryo lab counts as a hidden area if you use it before the red door is open.
  • Hydro Core: from the service ring, follow the passage towards the centre of the colony to reach the green-lit pump room under the tree.

Tips

  • Lost? Press M. The orange diamond is where you need to go.
  • F turns on fly mode, which is handy for sightseeing. P is photo mode.
  • Free explore (on the title screen) opens every door and skips the story.
  • If the game runs slowly, set Graphics quality to Medium or Low in Settings.
